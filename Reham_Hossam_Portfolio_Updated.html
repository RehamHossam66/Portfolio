About me

From designing objects to designing insight

I graduated from Helwan University with a degree in Industrial Design, where I learned to look closely at a problem before deciding how to solve it. The part I loved most wasn't the object — it was the research and the patterns underneath it. That's what pulled me into data.

Since then I've built six end-to-end projects: a Python analysis, a SQL Server star-schema model, three Power BI reports and a Tableau supply-chain dashboard. Each one takes a raw file, cleans and models it, and ends with a recommendation someone could act on.

My approach is shaped by my design background: I care about how something is understood, not just how it's calculated. Before I open a chart tool, I ask what decision this needs to support — and I don't consider an analysis finished until the answer is clear to someone who wasn't in the data with me.

I'm completing the DEPI Professional Data Analyst track and looking for my first entry-level Data Analyst role, as well as freelance analytics projects. I'm happy to walk through any case study in detail.

Superstore Star-Schema Model in SQL Server

Turning one flat sales CSV into a reusable relational model with views and stored procedures, so every KPI comes from one source of truth.

9,993 order lines1 + 4 fact + dimensions2 + 2 views + procedures
  • Staging table split into 1 fact and 4 dimension tables, with keys and FK indexes
  • CTEs, window functions, ROLLUP subtotals and CASE tiering
  • Two views and two parameterised stored procedures for KPIs and customer profiles
